Our Story

A Legacy of Flavour

Zafran was born from a single belief: that great food is a form of love. Founded in 2018 by Chef Arun Mehta, our kitchen draws from Mughal royal recipes and regional home cooking — blended into something entirely our own.

Every ingredient is sourced with intention. Our spices are stone-ground in-house each morning. Our bread is tandoor-fired to order. There are no shortcuts — only craft.
